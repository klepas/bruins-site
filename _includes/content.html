Gives the footnotes a "Notes" heading in place of Bruin's own, names the footnote links, and wraps tables in a box that scrolls. A screen reader's list of links shows only their names, so a reference is named "Note 2" rather than "2", and its backlink "Back to reference 2". The number comes from the reference's text and the note's place in the list, never from the id: a footnote written [^Castaneda] has "Castaneda" in its ids. A note referenced twice gets two backlinks, named "Back to reference 2, 1 of 2" and "2 of 2". Each replace and split matches kramdown's exact markup, so check them after a kramdown update. On German pages the labels are marked English, and the table gets the page's language back.
